About

I build the data foundations that make customer and audience systems actually work. Usually in places that have never had them before, and usually for problems the organization previously considered out of reach.

My career has moved between two sides of the same problem. On the measurement and infrastructure side, I have built the canonical datasets and audience data products that media companies and brands depend on to transact. That includes owning a $130M cross-platform measurement portfolio whose datasets became the trusted currency for industry-wide TV and digital transactions through a $768M acquisition. On the customer and people data side, I have been the operator who needs those systems to work. For the Obama 2012 reelection campaign I built the voter CRM, entity resolution, and audience segmentation infrastructure that powered targeting at national scale. The platform integrated behavioral, social graph, and voter data in real time across tens of millions of supporters, and the decision-support systems I built optimized roughly $500M in media spend, generating approximately $80M in savings. I have since done versions of that work inside two of the largest global institutions in financial services and insurance, building the identity resolution and customer data foundations that made precision targeting and decision-making possible where none existed before. Each crossing deepened the perspective the other side does not have. I understand what good measurement needs to do because I have been the operator who needed it. I understand what it takes to build an intelligent data-driven organization because I have built the infrastructure that makes it possible.

I think in systems and have a low tolerance for tool adoption dressed up as strategy. I consistently ask what happens after the first version works. I have built and led teams of 20 or more across every major role, with a track record of attracting talent across organizations and developing people into leadership. I have built the investment cases for programs organizations had never attempted, negotiated third-party data partnerships and software licenses, run RFPs, and managed delivery against those commitments.

I speak and write about the ethical dimensions of large-scale data and targeting systems, a thread that runs from before the Obama campaign through everything since. Business Insider named me a top 10 person in digital politics, and my work has been covered in the Times, Post, and Bloomberg.

Open to senior executive roles, advisory and fractional engagements, and speaking.

Services

Engagements range from a single evaluation or negotiation to a multi-year program, delivered directly or as fractional leadership embedded in your team.

Strategy & business case

  • Digital data & AI transformation business case development

    Building the funded, board-level case for data and AI investments an organization hasn't made before: scope, cost, risk, and the sequencing that moves a contested program to delivery.

  • CRM, platform & third-party data licensing economics

    Evaluating whether a CRM, platform, or third-party data license actually fits how the organization uses it — modeling the true cost per seat, per record, or per source — and running the RFP or renegotiation that fixes the gap when it doesn't.

  • Data monetization & pricing strategy

    Designing pricing models for data products, including multi-dimensional pricing tied to usage, access tier, and market coverage.

Architecture & governance

  • Governance workflow design & development

    Designing who owns each data attribute, how a record is matched, enriched, and approved, and how that process holds up across lines of business.

  • Entity resolution & identity architecture

    Building the matching logic and canonical record structures that let an organization count its own customers, accounts, or constituents for the first time.

  • Master data quality & deduplication methodology

    Finding and fixing duplicate and fragmented records at scale, then building the ongoing process that keeps the data clean after the cleanup project ends.

  • Governed historical reporting & time-series KPI architecture

    Designing point-in-time snapshots and segment history so a past metric can be reproduced and explained, not just a table restored.

  • AI governance & guardrail design

    Translating Risk, Compliance, and Architecture requirements into the technical controls — access, lineage, and approval — that make an AI system safe to put into production.

Vendor & commercial

  • RFP & procurement management

    Running vendor selection from requirements through contract, for CRM platforms, data warehouses, and third-party data sources alike.

  • Data source evaluation

    Assessing a prospective data partner or data product against its actual decision value, usable rights, and integration cost before it reaches a deal.

  • Data partnership & licensing negotiation

    Structuring the commercial terms of a specific data relationship: exclusivity, derivative rights, revenue share, and renewal leverage.

Delivery & leadership

  • Project & program management

    Running multi-year, multi-stakeholder data and AI programs from charter through delivery, across technology, risk, and business stakeholders.

  • Fractional & interim data leadership

    Serving as the interim data leader to keep a program or function moving during a search, reorganization, or leadership gap.

  • Data organization design & team build-out

    Structuring data and analytics teams, defining roles, and hiring the people who run them.

Enablement

  • Data & AI literacy training

    Training executives and staff to read, question, and act on data and AI systems responsibly, without needing to become technical specialists themselves.
